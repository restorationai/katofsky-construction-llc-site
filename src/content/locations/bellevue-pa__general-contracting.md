Renovating a home in Bellevue, PA usually starts with a conversation about what's underneath the walls, not just what's going on top of them. Many of the borough's housing stock dates to the early 1900s, built into hillside lots above the Ohio River valley, and that combination of age and terrain changes how a kitchen remodel, bathroom update, or full rebuild actually gets planned. Katofsky Construction LLC handles renovations, remodels, and general contracting for Bellevue homeowners who want the work done right the first time, including post-damage rebuilds after a covered loss.

## Why Bellevue Properties Need Specific Renovation Planning

Bellevue's narrow lots and hillside grading mean a lot of homes were built with retaining walls, raised foundations, or additions tucked onto whatever flat ground was available at the time. That's fine until you open a wall for a remodel and find knob-and-tube wiring, undersized joists, or a foundation that's settled unevenly over a century. Older plaster and lath construction, common throughout the borough, also behaves differently than modern drywall during any post-damage rebuild: it's heavier, it doesn't patch the same way, and matching existing texture takes more skill than swapping in a drywall panel. We walk these conditions with the homeowner before we price anything, so there aren't surprise change orders halfway through a kitchen or bath remodel.

## Our Renovations, Remodels and General Contracting Process in Bellevue

We start with an on-site walkthrough to scope the work, whether that's a full kitchen gut, a bathroom remodel, or rebuilding rooms damaged by fire or water. For Bellevue addresses, that scope includes checking what the borough's permitting process will require, since older homes often trigger electrical or structural review that newer construction wouldn't. From there we handle demolition, rough-in work, and finish carpentry as a single coordinated project rather than handing homeowners off between separate subcontractors. On post-damage rebuilds, we coordinate the reconstruction phase directly with whatever cleanup or abatement work came before it, so there's no gap between mitigation and the finished room.

## Permitting and Post-Damage Rebuilds in Bellevue

Borough permitting in Allegheny County municipalities like Bellevue tends to move at a predictable but deliberate pace, and homes near the hillside sections sometimes need additional review if a remodel touches load-bearing walls or changes drainage patterns. We build that timeline into the project schedule up front instead of letting it surprise a homeowner mid-renovation. When the work is a post-damage rebuild tied to an insurance claim, we document the scope in enough detail that most carriers can process it without repeated site visits, which keeps the rebuild moving instead of stalling on paperwork.

## Local note

A good number of Bellevue's homes sit on lots where the original structure and any later additions were built at different grades, which means a kitchen or bathroom remodel sometimes reveals a step-down in the subfloor that isn't visible until the old flooring comes up. We check subfloor elevation early in the process on these jobs so the new layout accounts for it instead of forcing an awkward transition between rooms.
