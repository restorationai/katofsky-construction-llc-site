## Why Bellevue Homes Need a Different Approach to Carpet

Bellevue sits on sloped terrain above the Ohio River valley, and a lot of the borough's housing stock dates to the early 20th century, narrow lots, shared walls, and carpet laid directly over original hardwood or old subfloor rather than modern padding systems. That matters for cleaning: aggressive hot-water methods that work fine on a newer home's synthetic pad can over-saturate a thin or compacted pad in an older Bellevue duplex, and it takes longer for moisture to evaporate through original wood subfloor than through today's engineered products. Pittsburgh-area humidity in late summer slows that evaporation further, which is part of why we calibrate dwell time and extraction passes to the age of the structure, not just the visible soil level.

## Our Carpet Cleaning Process

We start with a walkthrough to identify traffic patterns, pet or stain areas, and the carpet's fiber type, since wool and older nylon blends react differently to pretreatment chemistry than modern synthetic carpet. Heavy soil and traffic lanes get a pretreatment pass before the main cleaning step, and stubborn stains (coffee, pet accidents, rust rings from old radiators common in Bellevue's older homes) get spot-treated separately rather than relying on one blanket chemical across the whole room. We follow IICRC S100 cleaning guidelines for agitation, dwell time, and finishing, then groom the pile so it dries evenly and doesn't flatten into new traffic patterns. For commercial carpet cleaning in Bellevue's small storefronts and office suites along Lincoln Avenue, we schedule around business hours so foot traffic and drying don't collide.

## Carpet Sanitization and Stain Removal

Carpet sanitization matters most in households with pets, kids, or anyone managing allergies, and it's a separate step from basic cleaning: it targets what's living in the fiber, not just what's visible. We treat sanitization and stain removal as distinct jobs with distinct products, because a sanitizing agent strong enough to address odor sources isn't always the right chemistry for lifting a tannin stain or old pet discoloration. On deep carpet cleaning jobs we'll often run a pre-spray, a primary cleaning pass, and a post-treatment rinse so residue doesn't stay behind in the pile and attract soil faster the following month.
