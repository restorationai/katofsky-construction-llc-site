**Mold problem in a Greensburg home or building?** The older housing stock around Westmoreland County's county seat, combined with the region's humid summers and the clay-heavy soil common in the foothills of the Laurel Highlands, creates conditions where mold takes hold fast once a basement or crawlspace stays damp. Katofsky Construction handles mold remediation for Greensburg homeowners and property managers, working to the IICRC's remediation standards from containment through final clearance.

## Why Greensburg Properties See Mold Issues

Greensburg's housing stock runs older than a lot of the surrounding suburbs, with plenty of homes built between the 1920s and 1960s on stone or block foundations that were never designed with modern vapor barriers in mind. Combine that with Western Pennsylvania's humid continental climate, where summer dew points regularly sit high enough to keep basement air damp for weeks at a time, and you get a steady stream of mold calls tied to sump pump failures, grading issues on sloped lots, and roof or gutter problems that go unnoticed until a ceiling stain shows up. Westmoreland County's clay-dense soil also drains slowly after heavy rain, which means foundations that aren't properly waterproofed can stay saturated long after a storm has passed, feeding mold growth behind finished basement walls where nobody sees it until the smell gives it away.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Greensburg

We start with a moisture assessment, not a guess, because mold growth almost always traces back to a specific water source: a cracked foundation, a failed sump, condensation on a poorly insulated pipe, or a roof leak that's been slow-dripping for months. Once we've identified the source and the scope of the affected materials, we isolate the work area with physical containment and negative air pressure so spores don't migrate into the rest of the home while we remove compromised drywall, insulation, or flooring. Our crews follow IICRC AMRT protocols for mold-specific work, and because so many Greensburg basements are partially finished with carpet over concrete, we pay close attention to what's happening underneath flooring that looks fine from the surface. After remediation, we clean and treat remaining structural surfaces and coordinate any air quality testing the homeowner or their insurer wants before the space is closed back up.

## Greensburg Insurance & HOA Coordination

Mold claims in older Greensburg neighborhoods often hinge on whether the moisture source was sudden (a burst supply line) or gradual (a slow foundation seep), and that distinction matters a lot to how a carrier handles the claim. We document the source, the affected materials, and the remediation steps in detail so homeowners have what they need when a carrier asks questions about causation. For properties in homeowners' associations, particularly condo and townhome developments around Greensburg that share common walls or crawlspaces, we coordinate directly with property management on access and any notification requirements before containment work begins.

## Local note

A lot of Greensburg's older homes have stone or rubble foundations rather than poured concrete, and those walls breathe moisture differently than a modern foundation. Surface treatments that would fully dry out a newer basement can leave a stone foundation still releasing moisture into the air for weeks, which is why we extend monitoring time on those jobs rather than closing them out on a standard timeline. It's a detail that only shows up once you've worked enough of the pre-war housing stock in this part of Westmoreland County to know what to expect.
