**Lead paint abatement in Swissvale** almost always starts with a renovation decision, not a emergency call: a kitchen gut, a window replacement, or a porch rebuild on a house that went up before 1950. Once a contractor's saw or sander hits painted trim on one of these homes, the job stops being cosmetic and becomes a regulated lead-safe project. Katofsky Construction handles that transition for Swissvale homeowners and the contractors who hire us, testing first, containing second, and documenting everything so the work holds up if a buyer's inspector or a county official asks questions later.

## Why Swissvale Properties See Lead Paint Issues

Swissvale's housing stock reflects the steel-era growth of the Pittsburgh river valleys: dense blocks of frame duplexes, brick rowhouses, and single-family homes built roughly between 1900 and the 1940s, many on narrow lots with shared walls or tight setbacks. Exterior trim, porch railings, window sashes, and interior woodwork from that period were almost universally finished with lead-based paint before the federal ban took effect in 1978. Add Allegheny County's freeze-thaw winters, which crack and lift old paint film faster than milder climates, and you get a steady supply of chipping, chalking paint on sills, soffits, and stair risers, exactly the friction surfaces where lead dust becomes a hazard during routine wear, let alone a renovation.

## Our Lead Paint Abatement Process in Swissvale

Every project starts with testing, either XRF surface analysis or lab-submitted paint chip samples, so we know which components actually contain lead before anyone opens a wall. From there we isolate the work area with poly sheeting and negative-air containment, following EPA Renovation, Repair and Painting (RRP) work practice standards for dust control on pre-1978 housing. Removal methods are chosen by substrate: wet scraping and chemical stripping for trim we're keeping, controlled demolition and bagging for material coming out entirely. HEPA vacuuming and wet wipe-down close out each zone, and we run clearance checks before containment comes down, not after.

## Permitting and Lead-Safe Work in Allegheny County

Swissvale sits inside Allegheny County's building and health department jurisdiction, which means renovation permits for older homes can trigger lead-safe work practice requirements even on jobs that aren't full abatement. We pull the right permits before demolition starts and keep our RRP-compliant documentation on file, including waste disposal manifests, so homeowners aren't caught flat-footed if the property sells or a tenant complaint surfaces later. For landlords with rental units in Swissvale's older duplexes, this paperwork trail matters more than most people expect, PA's lead disclosure rules follow the property, not just the current owner.

## Local note

A lot of Swissvale's building stock sits on sloped lots that step down toward the Monongahela valley, which means porches, exterior stairs, and retaining-wall trim catch more direct weather than flat-lot homes do. We've found lead paint failure concentrates hardest on south- and west-facing porch components here, the sun-and-freeze cycle on those exposures breaks down old paint film faster than on shaded north walls, so that's usually where testing turns up the highest readings on an exterior job.
