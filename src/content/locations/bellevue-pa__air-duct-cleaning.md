## Why Bellevue Homes Build Up Duct Debris Faster

Bellevue's older duplexes and compact single-families were often retrofitted for forced-air heat well after the original construction, which means ducts frequently route through tight crawlspaces, old coal-chute chases, or unconditioned attic knee-walls. Those spaces trap humidity differently than a modern build, and that humidity settles into dust inside the duct run rather than venting out. Add in the borough's hillside lots, where homes sit close together and yards are small, and you get less natural airflow through crawlspaces, which keeps duct interiors damper longer after a humid stretch. Homes near the Ohio River flats tend to run slightly higher ambient humidity than the hill streets above Route 65, which shows up as a heavier dust load when we open a supply trunk.

## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process in Bellevue

We start by walking the system, register by register, to map where the trunk line runs and where branch ducts were added or rerouted, which is common in homes that have had additions or converted attic space. From there we use negative-air containment at the main trunk so loosened debris doesn't just migrate to the next room, then agitate and vacuum each run with HEPA-filtered equipment before sealing registers back up. On most Bellevue jobs we also inspect and clear the dryer vent run while we're in the system, since a restricted dryer vent often shares a chase with the main HVAC trunk in these older layouts and gets overlooked during a standard duct cleaning. Sanitization of the interior duct surface is available as a final step for households dealing with pet dander, smoke residue, or ongoing dust complaints, applied after the mechanical cleaning rather than as a substitute for it.

## Equipment & Methods We Use

Duct cleaning work uses source-removal methods: negative-air machines with HEPA filtration pull debris out of the system rather than just stirring it loose, and rotary brush or air-whip agitation tools break up compacted dust inside branch runs without damaging flex duct or sheet metal seams. We photograph before-and-after conditions at the main trunk and at a sample of registers so you have a record of what was actually inside the system, which matters if a property manager or an out-of-town landlord in Bellevue is asking for documentation on a rental unit.
