Brentwood's narrow lots and brick duplexes, many built in the 1920s and 1930s when the borough grew up as a streetcar suburb south of Pittsburgh, don't leave much margin for error during a kitchen gut or a post-fire rebuild. A renovation here has to work around original floor joists, shared party walls, and grading that slopes hard toward the back alley on half the streets in town. Katofsky Construction handles the full range, from a straightforward bathroom remodel to a reconstruction after fire or water damage, with crews who've worked the tight footprints typical of the South Hills.

## Why Brentwood Properties Need Renovation and Rebuild Work

A lot of Brentwood's housing stock predates modern electrical and plumbing codes by decades. When we open a wall for a kitchen remodel, it's common to find knob-and-tube remnants, cast iron drain lines, or framing that was never squared to begin with because the lot itself sits on a grade. The borough's hilly terrain also means water finds its way into basements and crawlspaces that weren't built with modern drainage in mind, which turns what starts as a cosmetic remodel into a structural conversation fast. Add in brick exteriors that trap moisture differently than vinyl or wood siding, and you get renovation jobs where the finish work is the easy part. The harder part is getting the bones right first.

## Our Renovation and General Contracting Process in Brentwood

We start with an on-site walkthrough that looks past the cabinets and fixtures to the structural and mechanical systems behind them. For Brentwood's older duplexes and single-family brick homes, that usually means checking party wall conditions before any demolition starts, since shared walls affect what we can and can't remove without engaging the neighboring unit's owner. From there we scope the work, whether it's a full kitchen remodel, a bathroom gut down to the studs, or a rebuild following fire or water damage, and sequence it so rough-in inspections happen before drywall goes back up. Allegheny County and Brentwood borough permitting requirements apply to most structural and electrical work, and we pull those permits as part of the job rather than leaving it to the homeowner to navigate separately.

## Brentwood Insurance and Permit Coordination

When a remodel follows a covered loss, documentation matters as much as the construction itself. We photograph and scope damaged materials before demolition so the record supports the claim, and we work directly with the adjuster on scope-of-work questions rather than leaving the homeowner to relay technical details back and forth. Brentwood's permitting runs through the borough and Allegheny County depending on the scope of the project, and older homes here sometimes trigger additional review if the renovation touches load-bearing walls or changes the footprint. We handle that coordination as part of the job, not as a separate line item the homeowner has to chase down.

## Local note

Because so many Brentwood lots back up to steep grades or retaining walls, drainage around the foundation often gets overlooked during a renovation until it shows up again the following spring. On rebuild jobs we check grading and downspout discharge as part of the scope, even when the original damage had nothing to do with water, because a remodel is the easiest time to fix a drainage problem before it becomes the next one.
