Millvale sits in the flood plain where Girty's Run meets the Allegheny River, and that geography shapes what a mold inspection turns up here more than almost anywhere else Katofsky Construction works. Basements and first-floor walls in this borough take on moisture in ways that rarely show up as an obvious leak, which is exactly why mold inspection and testing matters before a small musty smell becomes a bigger problem behind the drywall.

## Why Millvale Properties See Mold Issues

Millvale's housing stock is mostly early-to-mid-20th-century rowhouses and duplexes built close together on narrow lots, a layout left over from its mill-town years. Many of these homes have shallow or partially below-grade basements that sit near the water table, and the borough's documented history of Girty's Run flooding means a lot of foundations have absorbed water more than once over the decades, even if the most recent event was years ago. That kind of repeated wetting leaves masonry and framing more porous over time, so moisture wicks upward faster than it would in a newer, drier structure. Add Pittsburgh-area humidity swings between a wet spring and a muggy summer, and you get conditions where mold colonization can start within 24 to 48 hours of a hidden moisture source, long before anyone notices a visible patch.

## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Millvale

We start with a visual walkthrough focused on the areas Millvale homes are most likely to have trouble: sill plates, rim joists, basement corners, and any spot where a prior flood line is still visible on block or brick. From there we use moisture meters and, where useful, a thermal imaging pass to find damp pockets that aren't visible yet. Air sampling and surface swabs follow, calibrated against an outdoor baseline reading taken the same day, since spore counts shift with weather and we want a comparison that actually means something for this property, this week. Samples go to an independent lab, and we walk the homeowner through the results in plain language rather than a page of lab codes.

## Millvale Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most of the mold inspections we do in Millvale start because a homeowner is selling, buying, or has already had water intrusion and wants documentation before moving forward with remediation or a claim. We document findings with photos and lab reports formatted the way adjusters expect, which helps when a claim traces back to a covered water event rather than ongoing moisture, since that distinction often determines whether mold-related costs are covered at all. If a property is part of a condo or townhome association, we can provide the same report format the association's management company typically requests for shared-wall or shared-basement situations.

## Local note

One thing we watch for specifically in Millvale: older homes near the lower part of the borough, closer to the Run and the river, often have parged or painted-over masonry basement walls from past repairs after flooding. That coating can trap moisture against the block instead of letting it evaporate, so a wall can look dry and painted on the surface while the material behind it stays damp for weeks. A moisture meter reading at several points across that wall, not just one spot, is usually what catches it.
