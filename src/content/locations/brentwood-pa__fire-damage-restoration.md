## Why Brentwood Properties See Fire Damage Issues

Much of Brentwood was built out during the housing boom of the 1940s and 50s, and that era left the borough with a lot of attached and semi-attached duplexes on narrow lots, many still running original or early-upgrade electrical panels. Overloaded circuits and aging knob-and-tube remnants in attic spaces are a common ignition source we see across these South Hills boroughs, and because so many homes share a party wall or a common attic, a fire that starts in one unit frequently pushes smoke and heat damage into the neighboring half before anyone smells it. Hillside lots and narrow streets also mean fire crews sometimes have limited staging room, which can affect how much structural damage spreads before suppression is complete.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Brentwood

We start with emergency board-up and tarping to secure the structure and stop weather intrusion through burned-out windows or roof sections, which matters in Allegheny County's wet winters when an open roofline invites a second loss on top of the first. From there we assess what's salvageable versus what needs to come out: char and heat-warped framing, soot-saturated drywall, and smoke-permeated insulation typically get removed, while structurally sound framing is cleaned and sealed. Smoke damage restoration runs in parallel, since soot behaves differently depending on what burned, protein residue from a kitchen fire clings to surfaces differently than the oily film left by a synthetic furniture fire. Contents that can be saved go through our contents restoration and storage process while reconstruction is scheduled, and our crews coordinate directly with Allegheny County and borough permitting requirements when rebuild work touches load-bearing walls or roofing.

## Brentwood Insurance Coordination

Fire claims in older duplex housing often get complicated when damage crosses into a neighboring unit, since that can mean two separate claims and two adjusters working the same structure. We document room by room with photos and moisture or soot readings as we go, and we bill most major carriers directly so homeowners aren't carrying the full cost of cleanup and reconstruction out of pocket while a claim is still open.
