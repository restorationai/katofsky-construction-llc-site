Katofsky Construction LLC handles water damage, fire and smoke damage, storm damage, and the reconstruction that follows for homes and small commercial buildings in Rankin. That means the full job: extracting water or clearing fire damage, drying and documenting the structure, and rebuilding what has to come out, under one crew instead of a handoff between a mitigation company and a separate contractor.

## Restoration emergencies common in Rankin

Rankin sits along the Monongahela River in Allegheny County, and that river geography shapes a lot of the calls we get. Older basements and crawlspaces built close to the water table take on groundwater during heavy rain or spring thaw, and the borough's mix of early-1900s rowhouses and small frame homes often has aging drain lines that back up under sustained storms. Western Pennsylvania's freeze-thaw winters are hard on supply lines too: a pipe that's fine in December can split the first warm week in February once ice inside it expands and then lets go. Add humid summers that push mold growth fast in anything left damp more than a day or two, and you've got the pattern we plan around here.

## Services we provide in Rankin

**Water damage restoration.** Water in a Rankin basement rarely stays where it enters. It tracks along joists and settles under flooring, which means a dry-looking surface can hide a saturated subfloor. We extract standing water, pull moisture readings from the structure itself, and dry to that benchmark rather than guessing.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration.** Smoke behaves differently in a brick rowhouse with plaster walls than it does in a newer frame addition. It settles into porous plaster, into old woodwork, and into HVAC ductwork that can recirculate odor through the whole house if it isn't addressed as part of the scope.

**Storm damage repair.** Wind and heavy rain off the river valley can strip shingles, back up gutters on older rooflines, and drive water in around chimneys and flashing that have seen decades of freeze-thaw cycling. We board up exposed openings and tarp roofs to stop the damage from spreading before repairs start.

**Reconstruction.** Once a structure is dry or the fire scope is cleared, we rebuild: drywall, flooring, trim, and framing repairs, carried out by the same company that handled the mitigation, so the repair matches what the loss actually required.

## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Rankin

Much of Rankin's housing dates to the early 20th century steel-era boom, with brick and frame rowhouses, plaster-and-lath interiors, and a mix of basements and partial crawlspaces built close to the river's grade. Plumbing from that era is often galvanized supply line or cast iron drain, both of which corrode from the inside and fail without warning well before they look bad on the surface. Older interior finishes can also mean lead paint or asbestos-containing materials in plaster, flooring, or pipe insulation, which has to be accounted for before demolition starts, not after. Structural repairs and rebuilds in Rankin are permitted through the borough and fall under Pennsylvania's statewide Uniform Construction Code, so any scope involving framing, electrical, or plumbing needs to be pulled and inspected properly rather than treated as cosmetic work.
